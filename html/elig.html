Eligibility Checker

Welcome! This is a simple tool for checking patient eligibility.
  • 1. Upload your patient report (from Odoo or InstaHMS).
  • 2. Upload the Eligibility XLSX file from Openjet.
  • 3. Click Process to check eligibility.
  • 4. Click Export Invalid Rows to download non-eligible claims.
